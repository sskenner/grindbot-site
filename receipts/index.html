What it does

  • Your crew keeps doing what they do

    Write the job or address on the receipt, like now. Snap a photo into a shared folder on the phone.

  • It reads the receipt and the handwriting

    Supplier, date, total, how it was paid, and the job written on it.

  • It lands on the right job

    Paid at the counter becomes an expense. Charged to your supplier account becomes a bill. Either way it's tagged to the job in QuickBooks Online, photo attached, so job costs add up as you go.

What if it can't tell which job?

It doesn't guess. A receipt with no job, or one that could be two jobs, waits for you to pick the job before anything goes into QuickBooks.

Does it catch duplicates?

It flags a receipt that matches one already in QuickBooks: same supplier, same date, same amount.

What do I need?

QuickBooks Online with your jobs set up as customers (or sub-customers), and phones that take pictures. On QuickBooks Desktop? Ask on the call.
